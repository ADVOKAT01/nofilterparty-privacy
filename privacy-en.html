Privacy and data

Privacy Policy

This edition works offline and keeps game progress on your device. Read below for details.

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · Contact: goncharovandrey2016@yandex.ru

In brief: player names are optional; spoken answers are not recorded; this build has no ads or analytics; you can delete saved game data in the app.

1. Overview

This policy covers the Android game “Truth or Dare: 18+” (planned application ID: ru.nofilterparty.truthordare). The developer and privacy contact is the individual publishing this app. For privacy inquiries, email goncharovandrey2016@yandex.ru. This policy describes the current offline edition without advertising or analytics services.

2. Information used by the game

  • Player names or nicknames are optional. Default player names can be used instead.
  • Game and group preferences include the player count, optional gender selection for wording, level, language, sound, animation and comfort settings.
  • Game progress includes turn order, selected card identifiers and history, scores, streaks, timers, and an optional saved group.
  • Age notice acknowledgement is stored as a local setting; no birth date or identity documents are requested.

The game does not ask for phone numbers, addresses, identity documents, contacts or location. Spoken answers are not recorded or sent to the developer.

3. Purpose of processing

The information is used solely to run the local game, determine turns and cards, remember preferences, keep score, and restore a session after the app closes.

4. Storage

In the offline edition described here, game information is stored on the device using app-local storage (including Android Preferences and local WebView data). The developer does not receive server copies of player names, spoken answers or saved games. App-private storage should not be mistaken for additional encryption.

5. Internet access, external services and ads

According to the technical audit of this offline edition, it has no advertising or analytics SDKs, no in-app purchases, and no Android INTERNET permission. The core game works without a network connection. This policy will be updated before any release that introduces networking, advertising, or analytics.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you use Delete game data within the app or clear the app's data using Android settings. You can also use “Settings → Apps → Truth or Dare: 18+ → Storage → Clear data”. Uninstalling the app generally removes its app-local data; system backups and device transfer behavior depend on Android and device settings. The current technical project reports backups as disabled, but this must be rechecked in the final release APK.

7. Support emails

If you voluntarily contact support, the developer receives your email address and the message content needed to respond. Emails are handled through the mail provider and kept for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with the request and meet applicable obligations. Please do not send passwords, identity documents or unnecessary personal information. You may request correction or deletion of available support correspondence using the contact address.

8. Third-party services

In the offline edition described here, gameplay information is not transmitted by the app to the developer or third-party recipients. Your own email provider processes any support message according to its terms. This website is hosted using GitHub Pages, which may process technical visitor information under GitHub's own privacy policy. Website hosting is separate from the game itself.

9. Age restriction

The game is intended for adults 18 years of age or older. The app does not ask for a date of birth or identity documents.

10. Contact and updates

For data questions, corrections, or deletion requests concerning information available to the developer, email goncharovandrey2016@yandex.ru. The policy will be updated when data practices change materially; the revision date is shown above.
