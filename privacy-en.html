Privacy and data

Privacy Policy

The core game works offline. The Android edition with ads uses Yandex Mobile Ads and AppMetrica. This page explains the relevant data practices.

Draft revision: November 12, 2026 · Contact: goncharovandrey2016@yandex.ru

In brief: game names and saves stay on your device; spoken answers are not recorded; ads use Yandex third-party SDKs; declining advertising-related processing should not prevent core gameplay.

1. Overview and contact

This policy applies to the Android game “Truth or Dare: 18+” (planned package ID: ru.nofilterparty.truthordare). The developer and contact for privacy matters is the individual publishing the app. Privacy inquiries: goncharovandrey2016@yandex.ru.

The core game does not require an account or a continuous internet connection. However, the Android edition with ads includes third-party Yandex SDKs and may use the network for advertising-related functions.

2. Game data stored locally

  • Player names or nicknames: optional; default player names may be used.
  • Local participant IDs: the game generates a random identifier (UUID) for each player record to distinguish participants and keep track of game progress. It is not an account ID or advertising identifier.
  • Optional participant gender selection: may be stored in the local player record to adapt wording in game cards; it does not by itself indicate sexual orientation.
  • Preferences: player group, game mode, level, language, sounds, animations, comfort boundaries and selected restrictions.
  • Game progress: turns, card IDs and history, scores, streaks, timers and saved session state.
  • Advertising choice: the user's selection regarding advertising-related data processing may be saved in app settings.
  • Age notice: a local acknowledgement of the 18+ notice; no date of birth or identity document is requested.

The game does not record spoken answers or actions performed by players. Ordinary local gameplay does not require users to provide the developer with their real names, telephone numbers, addresses or contact lists.

3. Why local data is used

Names, preferences and progress are used to manage turns, choose suitable cards, keep score, enforce player-selected restrictions and resume games. These details are stored on the device in the app's local Android data, including Preferences and WebView storage. App-local storage does not itself mean that the data is additionally encrypted.

Third-party ad components have a separate purpose, described below. The developer does not operate a proprietary server that synchronizes game sessions.

4. Yandex advertising and AppMetrica

The Android edition is designed to show one small advertising banner using Yandex Mobile Ads SDK. AppMetrica SDK is included as a technical dependency of the advertising SDK. These components may contact Yandex services to request and display ads, measure advertising performance, perform diagnostics and address invalid ad activity.

Depending on SDK versions, configuration and permissions, the third-party components may process device and operating system details, app/SDK versions, network-related data including IP address, ad-display and ad-interaction events, diagnostics, and app or device identifiers. An advertising identifier may be used only where available and permitted by device settings and the app's permissions. AppMetrica documentation also lists crash and performance information among data that can be collected under default settings.

The app is not designed to send player names, spoken answers or saved game sessions to ad services as separate gameplay events. This does not exclude the processing of technical information by the third-party SDKs.

Further information: Yandex Mobile Ads SDK, AppMetrica data documentation, and Yandex Privacy Policy.

5. Your choices and offline access

The app offers a choice about advertising-related data processing before using the advertising feature. Declining does not prevent access to the core game, which remains available without an advertising banner. The choice can be changed through the app's advertising and privacy settings where that feature is available in the installed version. You can also contact the developer using the email above.

The main game works offline. Ads require network connectivity; without a connection, the game may operate without ads. Any required consent signals must be supplied to the Yandex SDK in accordance with applicable rules.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game preferences and sessions remain until you delete them in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app, subject to device settings. You can use the game's data deletion/reset feature where available, or open Android Settings → Apps → Truth or Dare: 18+ → Storage → Clear data. Android backup and device-transfer mechanisms may vary according to the final APK and device configuration.

Data already received by a third-party advertising provider is not necessarily deleted when you erase your local game. Its processing and retention are governed by the provider's applicable policies and law. You may contact the developer about information within the developer's control using the email above.

7. Support emails

If you voluntarily email support, the developer receives your sender address and message content to respond. Correspondence is retained by the mail provider for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request and comply with applicable obligations. Please do not send passwords, identification documents or unnecessary personal information. You may request correction or deletion of correspondence available to the developer via the same email address.

8. Third parties and website hosting

Advertising and technical information processed by the embedded SDKs may be transmitted to Yandex and relevant Yandex services and is handled under their applicable terms and privacy policies. Support correspondence is also processed by the relevant email provider.

This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical visitor information under its own privacy policy when the page is accessed. Hosting this page is separate from local game storage.

9. Age and voluntary participation

The game is intended for adults aged 18 and over. The app does not require identity documents or birth dates to create a game session. Participation in dares is voluntary, and players may decline unsuitable tasks.

10. Your rights and policy changes

For questions about processing, access, correction, restriction or deletion of information under the developer's control, contact goncharovandrey2016@yandex.ru. For data held by Yandex, additional procedures and rights may apply under Yandex's privacy policy.

This policy will be updated when SDKs, processing practices or app features materially change. The revision date appears above.
